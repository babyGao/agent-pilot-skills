## 1. The twenty top-level industry divisions (enumeration checklist)

China's GB/T 4754-2017 divides the economy into 20 lettered divisions. NAICS (US/Canada/Mexico)
and ISIC (UN) are structured the same way and can be walked identically.

**Go through all twenty. For each, ask: is there anyone in here who needs my job done?**

| Code | Division | Buyers you typically find here |
|---|---|---|
| A | Agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, fishery | Large growers, agri-input distributors, produce processors |
| B | Mining | Mine operators, aggregates, mining-equipment services |
| C | Manufacturing | **The largest division**, 31 major groups: food, textiles, furniture, plastics, metal products, machinery, electronics |
| D | Electricity, heat, gas and water supply | District heating, gas utilities, solar operators |
| E | Construction | General contractors, fit-out, municipal works, installation |
| F | Wholesale and retail | Wholesalers, distributors, retail chains, marketplace sellers |
| G | Transport, warehousing and postal | Freight, parcel, warehousing, forwarders, last-mile |
| H | Accommodation and food service | Hotels, B&Bs, restaurant chains, single sites |
| I | Information transmission, software and IT services | Software firms, systems integrators, data centres, agencies |
| J | Finance | Small lenders, guarantors, factoring, insurance brokers |
| K | Real estate | Developers, agencies, property management, park operators |
| L | Leasing and business services | Equipment rental, staffing, events, tax agents, consultancies |
| M | Scientific research and technical services | Testing and certification, design institutes, engineering consultancies, patent agents |
| N | Water, environment and public facility management | Environmental engineering, waste handling, municipal maintenance |
| O | Resident services, repair and other services | Domestic services, auto repair, laundry, weddings, beauty |
| P | Education | Training providers, vocational schools, courseware suppliers |
| Q | Health and social work | Clinics, dental, health screening, elder care, device distributors |
| R | Culture, sport and entertainment | Film production, gyms, live events, attractions |
| S | Public administration and social organisations | Procurement runs through tenders; cold email is largely ineffective |
| T | International organisations | Not applicable |

**Coding**: one letter (division) then two digits (major group), three digits (group), four digits
(class). Business registries index companies by these same codes, so **once you land on a class
code you can query the registry directly.** That is what turns "I thought of it" into "I can pull
the list."

Full four-level table: [China National Bureau of Statistics](https://www.stats.gov.cn/xxgk/tjbz/gjtjbz/201710/t20171017_1758922.html)
· [NAICS](https://www.census.gov/naics/) · [ISIC](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/isic)

## 2. Reading an input-output table

This answers "whose cost structure contains my line item."

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who publishes it | National statistics offices. China compiles a benchmark table every 5 years plus extension tables in between; the US BEA, Eurostat and OECD publish equivalents |
| Which quadrant | **Quadrant I** (intermediate input x intermediate use) — the inter-industry flows |
| How to read it | Down a **column**: what that industry consumes to produce. Across a **row**: where that industry's output goes |
| Key figure | **Direct consumption coefficient** — how much of your sector each other sector consumes per unit of its own output |
| How to use it | Find your own row, read across, and the large coefficients are your customer industries, already ranked |

**Industries where freight is a large share of cost share a signature**: bulky, cheap per unit,
fragile, or temperature-controlled. Plastics, building materials, furniture, glass and fresh
produce all qualify — which is why they buy transport heavily.

## 5. Where the demand signals are

| Signal | Where to look | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Job postings | Job boards, company careers pages | Five openings for data entry or quoting means the volume justifies hiring — **and the budget is already approved** |
| Repeated support answers | Storefront Q&A, published support scripts | The same question over and over means information is not landing |
| Recurring complaints | Product reviews, review sites, complaint platforms | The same complaint repeated is an unsolved problem |
| Forum and community questions | Vertical communities, trade groups | The same how-do-I repeated means no good tool exists |
| Tender notices | Public procurement portals | What they are actively spending on right now |
